Redwan Khan

Author & Social Development Researcher

About

Welcome to my official space. I am a published author and a seasoned social development researcher. My creative work centers deeply around the grass-roots human perspective, exploring themes of migration, endurance, and cultural heritage. Alongside my literary endeavors, I specialize in qualitative field operations, public health research frameworks, and community development.

Selected Works & Projects

  • Durba Ghaser Jauban (The Youth of the Bent Grass): A Novel of Exile
    An ongoing epic literary fiction manuscript exploring global displacement, migration, and the resilience of human survival.
  • Published Short Stories
    Notable solo short stories exploring regional narratives and deeply felt life perspectives, including **'ইহলৌকিক'** and **'জলডুমুর'**.
  • Mysteric Songs of Hajera Bibi
    A prose and research project preserving, evaluating, and documenting structural regional folk music compositions and oral histories.
  • Dakatia - The Seven Generation Adrift
    A deep narrative exploring ancestral timelines, grass-roots historical endurance, and survival.
