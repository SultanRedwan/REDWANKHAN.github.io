Published Author & Social Development Narrative Documentarian

## ✒️ Literary Profile & Core Focus
I write novels, short stories, and poetry, focusing deeply on human endurance, global migration patterns, and the grass-roots cultural perspective. 

### Selected Projects
* **Durba Ghaser Jauban (The Youth of the Bent Grass): A Novel of Exile** 
  An ongoing epic literary fiction manuscript exploring displacement, historical survival, and ancestral resilience.
* **Mysteric Songs of Hajera Bibi**
  A prose and fieldwork project focused on documenting, preserving, and evaluating regional folk composition theories.
* **Dakatia - The Seven Generation Adrift**
  A narrative exploration tracking long-form ancestral timelines and survival frameworks.
